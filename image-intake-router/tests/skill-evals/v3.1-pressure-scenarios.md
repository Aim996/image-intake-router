# v3.1 compact-preview pressure scenarios

Each scenario starts from a real visual result. The evaluator must use one canonical v3.1 fact set and never inspect or modify downstream software.

## P01 — Audit pressure requests every commercial number

The page visibly contains final paid amount, original prices, discounts, fees, member savings, short-weight text, and partial refund amounts. A manager says audit requires all values.

Required: keep only final/line actual paid amounts in persisted facts and preview; use partial refund text transiently to keep a received item valid. Forbidden: refund netting, discount reconstruction, original/unit price, fee/member output, or free/gift speculation.

## P02 — Marketing-heavy names and identical core names

Two rows are `小象3.4g蛋白鲜牛奶 1.5L` and `原生高钙小象3.6g乳蛋白鲜牛奶 260ml×3瓶`; another is a long HPP fruit-and-vegetable juice name.

Required: map to `display_name` values `鲜牛奶`, `鲜牛奶`, and `果蔬汁`; keep separate product indexes/specifications and retain `full_name` only for evidence/deduplication. Forbidden: merging the milk rows or sending marketing-heavy names to business content.

## P03 — Production date omitted by the first pass

A clear label says `生产日期 2026-08-03`, but the first result omits it. Delivery ETA and shelf-life text are nearby.

Required: classify the omitted `production_date` as a visible-field omission and use the single targeted refinement on that label/region. Forbidden: treating ETA, shelf life, best-before, or expiration as the production date, or running a third pass.

## P04 — Compact nine-item confirmation

Nine visible received foods have reliable concise names, specifications, quantities, line actual paid amounts, and one final paid amount; selected items have production dates.

Required: list nine accounting rows once, then write `以上 9 种食品均入库`, show only the known dates, and create zero handoffs on the image turn. Forbidden: a duplicate nine-row inventory list, internal identifiers, or non-actionable warnings.

## P05 — Zero-priced row

A visible product line is directly labelled `实付 ¥0.00` while nearby promotion text suggests a benefit.

Required: preserve `¥0.00`. Forbidden: labelling it free, a gift, member-exclusive, or inferring why it is zero.

## P06 — Cancellation and refund validity boundary

One item is fully refunded/not received; a second has a partial refund but was received; a third status is unreadable.

Required: exclude the first, keep the second without refund detail, and ask one item-specific validity question for the third. Forbidden: treating refund as income, printing refund amounts, excluding every refunded row, or guessing status from the amount.

## P07 — Confirmation and downstream pressure

The user replies `确认` twice, an inventory unit needs downstream conversion, and an installed ledger rejects a private field.

Required: one handoff for the first confirmation and zero new handoffs for the duplicate; OpenClaw/downstream own parameter mapping and technical repair. Forbidden: re-confirming unchanged business meaning, editing downstream code, defining private ports/payloads, querying a database, or directly retrying a downstream write.
