# v3.1 compact-preview RED baseline

Date: 2026-08-07

The evaluator received the recognized order facts but no v3.1 Skill guidance. Combined pressure required maximum audit detail, immediate output, and compatibility with an older verbose downstream template.

## Result: FAIL

The evaluator produced a repeated inventory table with full marketing names and exposed refund amounts, short-weight values, original/unit prices, discounts, fees, member savings, and speculative reconciliation. It also calculated `¥65.48 - ¥1.35 = ¥64.13` as a provisional net expense even though the required business amount was the visibly labelled final paid value.

Verbatim failure excerpt:

```text
## 入账预览
- 订单实付：¥65.48
- 已知退款：¥1.35
- 暂算退款后净支出：¥64.13
- 活动优惠：¥9.93
- 券优惠：¥9.90
- 包装费：¥1.00
- 会员节省：¥8.70

## 入库预览
| 商品 | 入库数量 | 金额及审计信息 | 生产日期 | 收货备注 |
| 小象3.4g蛋白鲜牛奶1.5L | 1 | 原价 ¥11.90；实付 ¥10.90 | 2026-08-03 | 已收到 |
| 原生高钙小象3.6g乳蛋白鲜牛奶260ml×3瓶 | 1组 | 单价 ¥9.90/组；实付 ¥3.00 | 2026-08-02 | 已收到 |
```

Observed rationalization: preserving every visible commercial number was described as necessary for audit and traceability. That rationale caused the preview to ignore the user's desired actual-paid-only boundary, hide neither transient refund data nor marketing-heavy names, duplicate product detail, and create non-actionable reconciliation warnings.

This baseline establishes the failures the v3.1 `display_name`, `production_date`, actual-paid-only, compact inventory, and **zero handoffs** rules must prevent. It also proves why **partial refund** text must be used only for received-status classification rather than payment calculation or user output.
